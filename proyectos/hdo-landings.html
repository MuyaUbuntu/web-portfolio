Project 01 · Bogotá, 2024

PRO PLAY

Make play look like serious work.

Pro.Play is a method created by Juan Prego and endorsed by PLAYMOBIL pro: teams model real problems with figures to find new answers. HD* brought a free, in-person workshop to Bogotá, led by Jaime Lastra, one of the few certified trainers in Latin America.

The challenge: sell the professional value behind a toy, on a single page.

Three challenges

Built like the landing’s own “¿Cómo funciona?” cards. Hover one and its figure pops up.

01

Break the prejudice that play isn’t serious work.

How the design answeredProof comes first: the client logos sit right under “Play like a pro”, and the trainer’s credentials get a block of their own.

02

Make a hands-on workshop understood on one page.

How the design answeredThe method becomes four cards (start playing, observe, extract insights, decide), then a video and the same call to action.

03

Speak PLAYMOBIL pro’s visual language.

How the design answeredPlain white figures modeled in Blender, outlined PRO PLAY type and a rounded 3D button that looks like a toy.

Six decisions

Every 3D object in the landing marks one choice. Pick one to see where it lives on the page.

The hero shouts: “Impulsa una cultura colectiva” in hand lettering, with the founder mid-gesture. Culture as energy, not a slide.

Leads start at the top, not at the bottom: four cards name the reasons to get in touch, each with its own 3D icon.

The last field reads “What’s your dream?” instead of “Message”. The form opens a conversation.

Inflated 3D numbers and a pun: “our story lasts 28 years, the rest is just tales”.
