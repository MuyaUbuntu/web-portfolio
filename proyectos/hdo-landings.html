02 · Pro Play · Bogotá, 2024

Make play looklike serious work

Pro.Play is a method created by Juan Prego and endorsed by PLAYMOBIL pro: teams model real problems with figures to find new answers. HD* brought a free, in-person workshop to Bogotá, led by Jaime Lastra, one of the few certified trainers in Latin America. The challenge: sell the professional value behind a toy.

Solve it by playing

The landing’s line was “when you have a problem, solve it by playing”. So here are its three challenges: drop a figure on each number to see how the design answered it.

Challenge 01

Break the prejudice that play isn’t serious work.

Proof comes first: the client logos sit right under “Play like a pro”, and the trainer’s credentials get a block of their own.

Drop a figure on 01 to unlock

Challenge 02

Make a hands-on workshop understood on one page.

The method becomes four cards, start playing, observe, extract insights, decide, followed by a video and the same call to action.

Drop a figure on 02 to unlock

Challenge 03

Speak PLAYMOBIL pro’s visual language.

Plain white figures modeled in Blender, outlined PRO PLAY type and a rounded 3D button that looks like a toy.
