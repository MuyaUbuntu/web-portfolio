02 · Approach

A conference thatdoesn’t feel like one

Happy Talks offers disruptive talks that spark reflection and drive change inside organizations. The page had to feel the same: authentic, memorable and far from corporate.

Less brochure, more backstage pass.

Goals

  • Present the speakers and their talks.
  • Generate leads with a clear path to a request.
  • Tell the story with an agile structure.

Scope

  • UX/UI, desktop and mobile
  • Visual language: type, color and stickers
  • Art direction, photography and illustration
  • 3D modeling of key elements
