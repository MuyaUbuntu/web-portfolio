01 · Challenge

Back to thefundamentals

PMI is moving towards a smoke-free, multi-category business. Its sales force had lost focus on core operational tasks, so the program had to rebuild ownership and execution at the point of sale.

2.5

days of intensive training, plus post-training follow-up.

1

shared vision for every collaborator: the “Perfect PMI Store”.

02 · Idea

Don’t sell smoke

In Spanish, “vender humo” means making empty promises. For a company leaving smoke behind, the phrase becomes a double message: deliver real results, and let the smoke go.

Letters made of smoke, about to disappear.
