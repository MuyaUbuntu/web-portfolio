Believe in our Behaviors

Boehringer Ingelheim defined five behaviors to guide its culture for the next ten years. Five abstract values are easy to agree with and easy to forget, so I proposed a character to carry all of them: Pillhavior, one friendly face with five moods.

01 · Challenge

Five behaviors,ten years to live them

Boehringer Ingelheim, the German pharmaceutical company, asked HD* for a campaign to launch its five new behaviors inside the company.

What hurt

The culture had to evolve, so people would live and identify with five behaviors over the next ten years.

The dream

Employees who understand the five behaviors and apply them in their daily work.

The task

A communication campaign to launch the five new behaviors.

02 · Strategy

A cultural narrativebefore a single drawing

HD* framed the campaign as a cultural narrative: six layers, from the company’s DNA to the symbols people meet every day. The character lives in the creative concept and in the symbols. Pick a layer.

03 · Meet Pillhavior

One pill,five behaviors

Pill plus behavior. In the story, he was born at the heart of the pharmaceutical industry in Germany, as the essence of the company’s DNA, with one mission: to make people believe in science and in the power of the five behaviors. Each behavior got a shape, a color and a pose. Your turn: live all five.

04 · Motion

Color for the slides,line for the screens

Besides the full-color key visuals, Pillhavior has a line version in the brand’s greens, animated in After Effects for screens and social.

05 · In the office

A character peoplemeet every day

How the system lives in the workplace: walls, elevators, desks, screens and team-building sessions. The campaign was used internally, and later team-building activities were built on the same concept.
