Piensa en Jumbo

Jumbo built playgrounds for four decades, then grew into urban furniture, outdoor gyms and inclusive play. The campaign asks the people who shape cities to go past “big”: everyday objects blown up to the size of a city, with real people living on them.

01 · Challenge

Known for swings,built for cities

Jumbo’s catalog had outgrown the playground: benches, outdoor gyms, sports surfaces, fountains and inclusive play. The market still saw a maker of children’s playgrounds. The campaign had to change that without losing the trust the brand had earned.

40+ years

known in Mexico for children’s playgrounds.

A wider catalog

Urban furniture, outdoor fitness, adventure zones and inclusive play.

New audience

Architects, developers and city governments, and the people who use the spaces.

02 · Idea

Don’t think big.Think Jumbo.

In Spanish, “jumbo” already means huge. The line pushes one step further than “big”: there is always a better way to live a space. Each poster pairs one verb with the same size, tamaño Jumbo, and speaks to a different audience.
