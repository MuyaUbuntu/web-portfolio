03 · Anatomy

Take a masterapart

Every slot visual master is the same stack of nine layers, each one with a fixed place and a fixed job. Drag the slider to pull them apart. The names are the real layer names in the file.

Layers
  1. Disclaimerauto-fit, min 14 px
  2. Copycopy component
  3. Logobrand component
  4. Logo shadowtinted from the image
  5. Slot logoofficial file, never redrawn
  6. Subjectduplicate for my cutout
  7. glow_linelight ribbon
  8. Mask groupdepth layer under the copy
  9. Backgroundfull image, 2k
04 · Reflow

One image,five placements

The resize pipeline moves every element to its stored position for each format. These are the real coordinates; the subject is never regenerated, only the image around it is extended.
