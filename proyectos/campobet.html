Part one · The work

One brand,every day

CampoBet publishes every day: slots, sports, seasons and offers, on social, paid, email, the blog and the app stores. My job was to make all of it look like one brand, and keep it coming. This part follows that path, from the new look to the pieces that carry it.

01 · The look

First, a lookof its own

The organic content had become monotonous. I proposed a new visual language and it was approved: more dynamic and sporty, built to break the routine of the feed. White leads, but intense green backgrounds and photographic art keep the rhythm changing, always within the brand colours. Drag the slider to see it change, stripe by stripe, like a lenticular print.

Part two · The system

The systembehind the volume

To keep that pace without losing consistency, I turned the brand rules into three AI production pipelines. I designed the zones, the art direction, the quality checks and the moments where a person decides. The AI renders inside those rules.

3pipelines
9layers per master

Automation · 01 · Anatomy

Take a masterapart

Every slot visual master is the same stack of nine layers, each one with a fixed place and a fixed job. Drag the slider to pull them apart, and tap a layer to hide or show it. The names are the real layer names in the file.
