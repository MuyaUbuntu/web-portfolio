    06 · The machine

    Pull the lever,watch the system work

    I designed three production pipelines that run as Claude skills, using Higgsfield for images and Figma for layout. I wrote the brand zones, the art direction rules and the quality checks, and I decided where a person has to step in. The AI renders inside those rules.
