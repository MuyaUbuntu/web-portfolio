01 · Challenge

A park nobodycould define

ArboTerra, a nature park in Puebla, had a problem: many families didn’t know what it was.

Families

Parents and kids, with very different reasons to visit.

Print + digital

Billboards and a steady stream of social content.

02 · Idea

Don’t define it.Let it be everything.

Leave the sentence open: ArboTerra es… adventure or magic for kids, learning for parents.

04 · The system

A toolkit,not a poster

The client’s team ran the execution, so I delivered a 35-page AdBook built to be reused.

Sorvette for the big words

Rounded block letters with small cut-in details, so every word feels hand-built.

Knewave for the call to action

05 · Results

Three years later,still ArboTerra es…

1st round

Concept approved at the first presentation.

In-house

The AdBook let the client’s team produce billboards and social on their own.

2023 → 2026

The tree, the lettering and #ArboTerraesMagia are still on the park’s Instagram, now produced by their own team.

The client wanted something timeless. These recent posts are made by ArboTerra’s in-house team, not by me. They’re here as proof the concept is still alive.

What I’d do differently

Give 3D the time it needs. A tree this complex in a few weeks was a stretch; today I’d prototype the scenes with AI before committing to the full model.
